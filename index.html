How It Works

Get your camera tested in three simple steps.

1

Allow Access

Click the “Start Camera” button. Your browser will ask for permission to use your webcam. Grant access to continue.

2

Check Preview

Once allowed, your live video feed appears instantly. Verify that the image is clear and your camera is functioning correctly.

3

Capture Snapshot

Happy with the preview? Hit the “Snapshot” button to freeze a frame. Right-click the image to save it to your device.
